2. Your own keyboard

How many keys does your keyboard have? The map shows where each note is. The orange key is Middle C, the key to find first (usually near the middle, just left of the 2 black keys).

Tap Listen, allow the microphone, then play one note at a time near your iPad. The page tells you which note it hears.

Optional: plug your keyboard into your computer with a USB cable, then connect. The map lights up the keys you play and shows their names. This works in Chrome or Edge on a computer, not on iPhone.

3. Learn the song, step by step

The notes below were read straight from your video. Pick a lesson (8 seconds of the song) and a hand. The page shows the next note, you play it, and it moves on. Use the 🎤 Listen button above, a USB keyboard, or tap Next.

4. Learn the keys

Tap a key to hear it and see its name. White keys are named A to G, then it repeats. The black keys sit in groups of 2 and 3. The white key just left of the group of 2 is always C.

Tap a key

5. What the colors mean

Blue notes: left handOrange notes: right hand

Falling bars show which key to press. A tall bar means hold the key. A short bar means tap it. Finger numbers: thumb is 1, then 2, 3, 4, and your little finger is 5.

6. Your first week

After week 1, do one new lesson every 2 or 3 days (right hand, left hand, then both). The song is 29 lessons long.
